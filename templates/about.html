About IL9Cast

Illinois 9th Congressional District Democratic Primary · March 17, 2026

Project Overview

IL9Cast was a real-time forecasting platform for the Illinois 9th Congressional District Democratic Primary on March 17, 2026. It aggregated live prediction market data from Manifold Markets and Kalshi, published a precinct-level election model covering 436 precincts with 100,000 Monte Carlo simulations, and collected snapshots every 3 minutes throughout the race.

The site now operates as a static archive. Historical prediction market data, the precinct model, and fundraising analysis remain accessible. See the Model page for how the forecast compared to actual results.

Disclosure: The author completed volunteer work for the Biss campaign. This did not influence the data or methodology presented here.
