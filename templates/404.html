Well… time to update your prior on whether Ryan is a good programmer.

Bayes' Theorem

P(H|E) = P(E|H) · P(H)P(E|H) · P(H) + P(E|¬H) · P(¬H)

where H = "Ryan is a good programmer" and E = "you just got a 404"

P(H) 50% Your prior belief that Ryan is a good programmer
P(E|H) 91% Chance of a 404 if he IS a good programmer
P(E|¬H) 60% Chance of a 404 if he is NOT a good programmer
Posterior
60.3%
P(H|E) = 0.91 × 0.500.91 × 0.50 + 0.60 × 0.50 = 0.603

Still more likely than not. Everyone ships bugs sometimes.
